## Overview

AutoShortsAI is an open-source desktop application designed to automate the process of creating engaging short-form videos using artificial intelligence.

Instead of manually reviewing hours of footage, AutoShortsAI analyzes video content, identifies meaningful moments, generates narratives, and prepares clips ready for publishing on platforms such as:

- YouTube Shorts
- TikTok
- Instagram Reels

The project is built around a modular architecture, making it easy to extend with new AI models and workflows.

# Project Goals

AutoShortsAI aims to become a free and open-source alternative to commercial AI video clipping platforms.

The long-term vision is to provide creators, educators, developers, and businesses with a transparent and customizable workflow for AI-powered short-form content creation.
